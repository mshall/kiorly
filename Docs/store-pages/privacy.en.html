Privacy Policy

Draft of 23 November 2026. Effective date: [to be set at publication]

This policy explains how the Kiorly mobile app (the "App") handles personal data. Kiorly is a staff app for clinics, pharmacies and laboratories (each a "Provider") that use Kiorly Clinic, Kiorly Pharmacy or Kiorly Lab. It is operated by [Kiorex legal entity name, registration number and address] ("Kiorex", "we").

1. Who is responsible for what

Two different roles apply, and we keep them apart.

  • Your Provider is the controller of patient and clinical data. The clinic, pharmacy or lab decides why and how patient records are used. Kiorex processes this data only on the Provider's behalf and on its instructions, as a processor. If you are a patient and want to see, correct or delete your record, contact the Provider first.
  • Kiorex is the controller of staff account and security data that we need to run the App: your sign-in, your devices and the security of your sessions.

The exact split is also set out in the data processing terms we sign with each Provider. [Counsel to confirm the controller/processor allocation and the wording of the data processing terms.]

What we do not collect. No advertising identifier, no contacts, no photo library, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Health information is not sent to analytics because there is none. Push messages never contain clinical values.

3. Biometrics stay on your device

If you lock the App with Face ID or a fingerprint, the check is done by your phone's operating system. Kiorly receives only a yes or no answer. Your face or fingerprint data is never read by, sent to, or stored by Kiorly or Kiorex.

4. How we use data

  • To provide the App and the Provider's workflows.
  • To protect accounts and patients: session security, device revocation, safety alerts.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. We do not use patient data to train AI models. [Counsel/founder to confirm this statement matches the AI gateway policy before publication.]

7. Retention

  • Staff account data: kept while the organisation's account is active, then deleted or anonymised within [X days] of closure, unless law requires longer.
  • Device records and push tokens: deleted when you or an administrator sign the device out.
  • Patient and clinical records: kept as long as the Provider decides and the law requires. Medical record retention periods are set by the Provider and Egyptian regulation.
  • On-device offline cache: removed on sign-out.
  • Security logs: kept [X days]. Logs contain no clinical content.

8. Your rights

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 gives people rights over their personal data, including to know what is held, to access it, to correct it, to have it erased or restricted where the law allows, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent given earlier. We are following the law and its executive regulations and will honour the rights it grants. [Counsel to confirm current status of the executive regulations, licensing and any data protection officer requirement.]

How to use them: for your own staff account, write to support@kiorly.com. For patient records, contact the clinic, pharmacy or lab that treated you; we will help the Provider answer your request. Account deletion for staff is carried out through your organisation's owner, and you may also ask us directly. You may also complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Centre.

9. Children

The App is for working staff and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not create accounts for children. Records of child patients are held by the Provider and handled under this policy as patient data.

10. Security

Sessions are tied to your device, tokens sit in the secure store of your phone, access depends on your role, and administrators can sign out any device. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify the Provider and, where the law requires, the authority and affected people.

11. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date. For material changes we will tell Providers in advance.
